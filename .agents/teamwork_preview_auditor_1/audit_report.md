## 3. Detailed Forensic Observations & Empirical Evidence

### 3.1 Source Code Analysis (`assets/blender_map/*.py`)

1. **`terrain_hydrology.py`**:
   - Computes analytical elevation over $200\text{m} \times 200\text{m}$ via `compute_terrain_elevation(x, y)` combining Northern Alpine ridges ($\sin/\cos$ wave interference up to $35.5\text{m}$), rolling foothills, lake basin depression at $(-40, -40)$ using smooth Hermite blending ($1 - 3t^2 + 2t^3$), and river spline carving with 120 sampled points.
   - Elevation delta is $\Delta Z = 35.05\text{m} \ge 15.0\text{m}$.
   - Generates mesh data via `mesh_terrain.from_pydata` on a $160 \times 160$ grid (25,600 vertices, 25,281 quad polygons).
   - Generates float vertex color attribute `COLOR_0` on the point domain, calculating procedural biome weights (sand, soil, grass, rock, snow) based on elevation and slope normal gradients.
   - Creates physically based water shader `M_Water_PBR` with Transmission Weight 0.92, IOR 1.333, and procedural micro-ripple bump map (`ShaderNodeTexNoise` + `ShaderNodeBump`).
   - All polygons have `poly.use_smooth = True`.

2. **`flora_generator.py`**:
   - Implements 4 distinct botanical species from scratch via procedural bmesh operations:
     - `Flora_Conifer`: 6 vertical trunk slices, 8 radial segments, and 4 tiered conical skirts with scalloped droop.
     - `Flora_Broadleaf`: Lofted organic curved trunk with 7 cross-sections and 4 overlapping volumetric canopy lobes.
     - `Flora_Reed`: 8 arching ribbon blades and 2 upright cattail stalks with brown velvet seed cylinders.
     - `Flora_Lily`: Notched floating pad with 8 sculpted petals.
   - Multi-material slots for bark/needles, bark/leaves, green/brown, pad/petals.
   - 100% of faces have `poly.use_smooth = True`.
   - Distributes 180 linked instances (`obj.data = prototype`) based on biome elevation, lake distance, and river proximity.

3. **`fauna_generator.py`**:
   - Highland Red Stag (*Cervus elaphus*):
     - Skeletal armature with 26 edit bones: `Root`, `Pelvis`, `Spine`, `Chest`, `Neck`, `Head`, `Jaw`, `Antler.L/R`, `Shoulder.L/R`, `UpperArm.L/R`, `Forearm.L/R`, `Hoof.L/R`, `Tail`, `Hip.L/R`, `Thigh.L/R`, `Shin.L/R`, `Hoof_Hind.L/R`.
     - Quad-dominant bmesh torso rings, branching antlers, and 4 limbs.
     - Skinned to 26 vertex groups matching bone names with normalized weights.
     - Active actions: `Stag_Idle` (60 frames: chest expansion breathing, vigilance head scan, tail twitch) and `Stag_Walk` (40 frames: 4-beat gait cycle across 4 limbs, spine counter-oscillation).
   - Golden Eagle (*Aquila chrysaetos*):
     - Skeletal armature with 16 edit bones: `Root`, `Pelvis`, `Spine`, `Chest`, `Neck`, `Head`, `Beak`, `Tail`, `Shoulder.L/R`, `Wing_Arm.L/R`, `Wing_Forearm.L/R`, `Wing_Tip.L/R`.
     - Aerodynamic fuselage and tapered airfoil wings.
     - Skinned to 16 vertex groups matching bone names.
     - Active actions: `Eagle_Glide` (60 frames: thermal soaring, banking) and `Eagle_Flap` (30 frames: downstroke / upstroke wing flapping).
   - Dual animation architecture: active action for viewport playback + NLA tracks pushdown with `use_fake_user = True` for multi-clip glTF export.

4. **`assemble_ecosystem.py`**:
   - Organizes the scene into 6 collections: `Terrain`, `Water`, `Flora`, `Fauna`, `Lighting`, `Camera`.
   - Directional key sunlight (energy 4.5, warm golden color) + Nishita sky dome texture (multiple scattering).
   - 45mm scenic camera elevated at $(65, -95, 42)$.
   - Exports both `ecosystem_map.blend` and `ecosystem_map.glb`.
