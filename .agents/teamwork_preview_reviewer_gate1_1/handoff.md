   - Sharp ridges (arêtes) and scree aprons are mathematically formulated in the elevation model.

3. **Subterranean Karst Cave Invariants & Critical Defect (Item 3)**:
   - Geotechnical clearance: 433 raycast samples confirm the rock thickness between cavern roof and mountain surface is $\ge 12.244\text{m}$, satisfying the geotechnical requirement.
   - However, the requirement specifically mandates an "arched entrance portal" connecting to the cavern.
   - The implementation generated 6 solid cubes nested together, leaving the terrain uncarved and the cave completely sealed.
   - Furthermore, `CAM_16` was placed outside the cavern ceiling, rendering a near-black wall of rock, while the verification script asserted a contrast ratio of $3.09 \ge 2.0$ caused by a corner light leak. This constitutes a facade implementation and self-certifying verification artifact (Integrity Violation).
   - Conclusion: Item 3 violates architectural completeness and review integrity constraints.

4. **Continuous Hydrology & Marine Bay Defect (Item 4)**:
   - Central lake containment is proven with 0 perimeter breaches and positive freeboard margins.
   - Cascades and meander stages 1–3 connect topographically into the central lake.
   - However, `Water_Bay_Marine` is unclipped and overhangs the 160m diorama block by $8.0\text{m}$ in mid-air, violating the monolithic diorama slab boundary.
   - The required "transparent water cutaways" from sea level to seabed are absent; the water is an unclipped horizontal plane with no vertical volume cutaway faces.
   - Conclusion: Item 4 fails visual and geometric boundary criteria.
